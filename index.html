Department of Dravyaguna
Tulasi
Botanical Name :

  • Ocimum sanctum Linn

    • Family Name :

  • Lamiaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Tulasi

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi –Tulsi

  • English – Holy basil

    • Synonyms :

  • Surasā

  • Vrindā


    • Habitat :

  • Tulasi is found throughout India.Commonly cultivated in front of the house and worshipped


    • External Morphology :

  • Leaf : Simple,opposite,short petioled,exstipulate,ovate,serrate,acute,gland dotted and has unicostate reticulate venation

  • Flowers : Pedicellate,bracteates,bracts are small and caduceus,bisexual,zygomorphic,complete,purple and hypogynous

  • Fruit : Schizocarps,carcerulus,four nut lets developed


    • Useful Parts :

  • Patra (Leaf)


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – katu Tikta(Pancarasa)

  • Guna –Laghu Ruksa

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya –Usna


    • Pharmalogical Actions :

  • Antiasthmatic

  • Antirheumatic


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Visama Prayoga : Juice of Tulasi taken with powder of Marica is very useful in Visama jwara

  • Kasa: Juice of black Tulasi if taken will honey alleviated Kasa originated by Kapha dosa


    • Dose

  • Swarasa (Juice ): 10 – 20 ml

  • Bija curna (Powder): 1 - 2 g


    • Formulation:

  • Surasadi kwatha

  • Tulasi Swarasadi taila
